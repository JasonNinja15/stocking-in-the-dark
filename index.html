EMPLOYEE ORIENTATION

Trucks back into Bay A and Bay B through the night. Walk into the trailer, pick up boxes and stack them on the empty shelves. Shelve enough boxes before 6:00 AM to meet the quota, or you're fired. Every night is harder than the last.

The building is dark. Your flashlight is all you have, and its battery runs down. Recharge it at the charging dock between the bays. The first-aid cabinet beside it can patch you up twice a night.

Not everything in here is freight.

• Boxes that move. If a box twitches or scratches when you get close, don't pick it up. Q kick it first. Kicking a fragile box breaks it, though. A kicked-out mimic hides as a box again somewhere on the floor or the shelves. Click your flashlight off and on at it to scare it off for good.
• Trailers that breathe. Check the manifest (top right) for a weight mismatch, and listen before you go in. Something sleeps behind the blankets at the back. Get too close and it sits up. Keep your light off it, or it growls and then comes for you. In the dark it only moves while you aren't looking at it. Use the panel beside the bay door to send a bad truck away.
• Something on the ceiling. Some trailers hold a thing that bolts out and climbs the walls. Listen for tapping overhead, and shine your light up at it. If the tapping stops right above you, move.
• The dark. Something walks the aisles where the lights are out. It only moves when no light is on it. Turn around often. Working lamps keep it back… while they work.

Later nights bring more. A night manager who writes up anyone standing around empty-handed (three write-ups and he comes for you). A shrink-wrapped figure that hunts by sound: walk, don't run. A truck driver who passes for a crewmate but makes no footsteps. Shelf slots that bite. A loudspeaker voice that isn't quite the supervisor's, with a wrong chime. And something out in the yard you shouldn't look at when a bay door jams open.

Between nights your crew's pay buys warehouse additions (more work, more time, new things living in them) and equipment. Items are on 1–9, the red headlamp on H. Hold 5 for the thermal scanner.
