EMPLOYEE ORIENTATION

Trucks back into Bay A and Bay B through the night. Walk into the trailer, pick up boxes and stack them on the empty shelves. Shelve enough boxes before 6:00 AM to meet the quota, or you're fired. Every night is harder than the last.

The building is dark. Your flashlight is all you have, and its battery runs down. Recharge it at the charging dock between the bays. The first-aid cabinet beside it can patch you up twice a night.

Not everything in here is freight.

• Boxes that move. If a box twitches or scratches when you get close, don't pick it up. Q kick it first. Kicking a fragile box breaks it, though. A kicked-out mimic hides as a box again somewhere on the floor or the shelves. Click your flashlight off and on at it to scare it off for good.
• Trailers that breathe. Check the manifest (top right) for a weight mismatch, and listen before you go in. Something sleeps behind the blankets at the back. Get too close and it sits up. Keep your light off it, or it growls and then comes for you. In the dark it only moves while you aren't looking at it. Use the panel beside the bay door to send a bad truck away.
• Something on the ceiling. Some trailers hold a thing that bolts out and climbs the walls. Listen for tapping overhead, and shine your light up at it. If the tapping stops right above you, move.
• The dark. Something walks the aisles where the lights are out. It only moves when no light is on it. Turn around often. Working lamps keep it back… while they work.

Later nights bring more. A night manager who writes up anyone standing around empty-handed (three write-ups and he comes for you). A shrink-wrapped figure that hunts by sound: walk, don't run. A truck driver who passes for a crewmate but makes no footsteps. A loudspeaker voice that isn't quite the supervisor's, with a wrong chime.

The roof. A ladder in the south-west corner climbs to the roof, where it is snowing hard and only your flashlight shows anything. A supply crate up there gives one free item a night. The ventilation plant is in the room in the middle of the roof (its door faces the hatch). From night 3, every couple of nights the warehouse starts getting cold: you get two minutes to get up there and hold E on the plant before the cold sets in and starts hurting everyone. A heater from the shop buys an extra night between cold spells.

Robots. Two warehouse robots can be bought: the small one takes a box from its pad on the dock and shelves it for you, the big one takes a pallet you set on its pad with the forklift up to a top shelf. Both are slow, carry one load at a time, and stop for anyone in their way.

Difficulty is picked on the title screen: Easy has smaller quotas, softer monsters and rarer bad luck; Hard has bigger quotas, harder hits and much more bad luck. A crew plays on the host's setting.

Power outages. From night 3 the mains can fail at any moment and stay dead until morning: every lamp goes out and the charger works ten times slower, unless the generator has fuel. And something that lives in the dark comes out: it can't see you, but it hears you, and it feels footsteps close by. Stand still.

Between nights your crew's pay buys warehouse additions (more work, more time, new things living in them) and equipment. Items sit on the hotbar at the bottom of the screen: scroll (or a number key) to pick one, right click or Space to use it. The red headlamp is on H, and the shake flashlight is held on R. Hold right click (or Space) with the thermal scanner picked to use it. The THIS WAY board on the dock wall points to the roof ladder and to everything you have bought. Press Done when you're finished shopping: the night starts once more than half the crew is done. The run saves after every night; Continue on the menu picks it up, and it's deleted when the run ends.

W A S D move · Shift run · Mouse look · F flashlight · E pick up / shelve / use (hold at the charger) · Q kick · Scroll pick item · Right click use item · G set box down · Esc pause (these are the default keys: change them, and the crosshair, in Settings)

Crews: one person presses Host a Crew and shares the five-letter code. Up to ten people can work a shift together. In the crew room the host can make the crew public, and then anyone can find it under Join Public and join, even mid-shift. The quota grows with the crew. If you go down, you watch the others in third person until the night ends: move the mouse to look around, click or use the arrow keys to switch.
